#@ tasks
One-pager builder: a few fields about the buyer's opportunity give a clean printable one-page summary they can send on.
Objection sorter: the buyer ticks the doubts they have and gets a plain answer and one question to ask any provider for each.
Vendor question list: the buyer's priorities give a printable list of questions to ask before signing with any provider.
Payback calculator: setup cost, monthly cost, and monthly gain give the month the buyer breaks even, shown on a timeline.
Priority sorter: a list of problems rated by cost and effort gives a two-by-two grid and the first one to tackle.
Capacity planner: current volume, growth rate, and staff hours give the month the buyer's team runs out of capacity.
Missed opportunity counter: inbound volume, response time, and close rate give how many deals or bookings slip away each month.
Pipeline health check: deals by stage and age give a list of stalled deals and the total value at risk.
Process cost breakdown: each step of a workflow with time and wage gives the cost per case and the most expensive step.
Scenario comparer: three versions of a plan with the buyer's own numbers give a side-by-side table of outcomes.
Self-audit checklist: a grouped checklist with notes per item gives a printable summary of gaps, saved in the browser.
Fit quiz: eight multiple-choice questions give which of three approaches suits the buyer and why.
Meeting prep sheet: the buyer's goal and limits give a one-page agenda and a list of numbers to bring.
Response time simulator: enquiries per hour and reply delays give the share answered within five minutes, one hour, and one day.
Hire or automate calculator: a role's salary, ramp time, and workload against a tool's cost give the cheaper path at 6, 12, and 24 months.
Document checklist: the buyer's stage gives a list of documents to prepare before a deal or rollout, grouped and ticked off in the browser.
Follow-up cadence planner: a lead source and deal size give a dated sequence of follow-up touches, printable.
Risk register: risks with likelihood and impact give a ranked table and a three-by-three heat grid.
Seat comparer: the same work done for a fee or for a share gives cash now against value later, with the bad case for each and the point where the share wins.
Downside check: a plan's cost, likely gain, and odds give the worst case, the expected result, and whether the loss is capped.
+ Churn cost calculator: customers, monthly loss rate, and average value per customer give the revenue lost each year and what a one-point cut in the rate would save.
+ Rework cost calculator: cases per month, error rate, and cost to fix each error give the monthly cost of mistakes and the saving if the rate fell by half.
+ Price rise tester: current price, volume, and margin give how much volume the buyer could lose after a price rise and still earn the same profit.
+ Tool overlap finder: the buyer's software tools with cost and main job give a list of overlaps and the yearly spend on duplicate tools.
+ Handoff counter: each step of a process with who does it gives the number of handoffs between people and the waiting time those handoffs add.
+ Cash gap estimator: monthly costs, billing terms, and typical payment delays give the cash the buyer needs to cover the gap between paying out and getting paid.
