Risk register for one deal

The Signed Gathering: one deal, checked against your mandate

A gathering is a set of folded sheets sewn as one unit. Each sheet below is one checked assumption. Every row carries the name of the person who checked it and the date.

Order of sheets: 1 the brief's assumptions, 2 two questions, 3 mandate tests, 4 definitions, 5 register, 6 heat grid and ranked table, 7 three cases, 8 AI review cost, 9 co-invest beside direct, 10 sign-off. The worked example is an industrial services carve-out with a $15M ticket.

SHEET 1

Assumptions in the brief, checked

  1. The reader wants a risk register. CHECKED
    They want one deal read against their mandate. The register follows the mandate tests.
  2. "Likelihood" means the same thing to everyone. FALSE
    It does not. Sheet 4 defines it as the chance the risk lands inside the hold period.
  3. "Impact" means the same thing to everyone. FALSE
    Sheet 4 defines it as loss on your ticket, in percent and in dollars.
  4. A coloured cell is a judgment. FALSE
    A likelihood stays empty until a document is attached. A risk with no document sits in the grey strip, outside the grid.
  5. Fit can be partial. FALSE
    Fit means four of four mandate tests pass. Three of four is a miss.
  6. Direct is the only path. FALSE
    Co-invest beside a lead sponsor runs on the same inputs. Sheet 9 shows both.

SHEET 8

Diligence cost with and without the AI pass

Direct path. Figures in $k.
PathBase caseBad case

The AI pass does

  • Indexes the data room.
  • Extracts terms from customer and supplier contracts.
  • Flags change-of-control, assignment, and indemnity clauses.
  • Drafts first-pass summaries for each folder.

A person still reviews

  • Named counsel reads every flagged clause and signs the legal red-flag list.
  • The CFO reviews quality of earnings and the debt terms.
  • A person attaches each document to its risk row before a likelihood is set.
  • The redo share in Sheet 7 covers the parts people must repeat.
Without AI = Full diligence With AI = Full diligence − First-pass review + AI pass + Redo share × First-pass review
