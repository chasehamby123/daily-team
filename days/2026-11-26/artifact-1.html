Permanent First, Five Views

A 1-page list of 15 questions to ask a lead-response provider before you sign. Sort first. Some answers you can change next week. Some you live with for the full term. Slow down only on those.

Permanent: red border, signature line.
Contract term, auto-renewal, rate lock, who owns consent records, who keeps the phone numbers, data return on exit.
Reversible: plain box.
Scripts, call hours, first sentence, handoff timing, state rule setup. Fix these after you sign.

Two questions about your agency

leads waited = leads/month × months

Cases below add the months until the gap closes.

Price block: flat fee next to per booked appointment

Enter the figures a vendor quoted you. The example figures are for illustration only.

appointments/month = leads/month × booked %
per-appointment cost = appointments/month × $/appointment
flat committed = $/month × minimum term months

Shortcut next to the usual path

The renewal risk sits with the shortcut. The rate-lock question is in the permanent column for that reason.

Contract read: with and without an AI reader

An AI reader checks a draft against the permanent column: term length, auto-renewal, rate changes, data ownership, consent records, phone numbers. Counsel then reads what it flags.

without AI = vendors × full-read hours × $/hour
with AI = vendors × (flag-read hours × $/hour + AI $/vendor)

A person still does these steps: counsel reads every flagged clause and the full permanent sections, then signs the sheet footer. You sign each red box. You run the phone test yourself. The reader finds clauses. It does not judge them.
