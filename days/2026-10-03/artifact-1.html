How this is calculated

Pace per kilometer:

pace_per_km = total_seconds / distance_km

Pace per mile converts using 1 mile = 1.609344 km:

pace_per_mile = pace_per_km × 1.609344

Each race projection assumes you hold your entered pace steady for the whole distance:

projected_time = pace_per_km × race_distance_km

Race distances used: 5K = 5 km, 10K = 10 km, half marathon = 21.0975 km, marathon = 42.195 km.

This is a flat-pace projection, not a prediction. Real race times are affected by fatigue, terrain, pacing strategy, weather, and fitness changes between distances. Longer races are usually run at a slower pace than shorter ones in practice.
