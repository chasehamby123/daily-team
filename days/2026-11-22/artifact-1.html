The Pass

Put each deal problem on the pass. Up is what it costs every month it sits. Right is the work to clear it. The ticket nearest the top-left corner goes out first.

Five inputs: two questions about the deal, three problem tickets. Drag a dot to place a ticket. A focused dot moves with the arrow keys.

The stack

Type 40m, 750k or 40,000,000. A cheque of zero closes the pass.

Formulas

  • Debt = deal − sponsor equity. Senior = min(debt, 60% × deal). Mezz = debt − senior. The 60% senior cap is a page assumption.
  • Cost per month = $250k × (1 − height ÷ 400), rounded to $5k. Effort = 12 weeks × across ÷ 400, rounded to 0.5 week.
  • Order = √((effort ÷ 12)² + (1 − cost ÷ $250k)²). Distance to the top-left corner. Smallest goes out first.
  • Six-month slip on one ticket = cost per month × 6.
  • Problem carry, bad case = Σ open cost per month × 6. Base case: tickets cleared one at a time in pass order, each runs until its own fix ends, months = weeks ÷ 4.33. Good case: all worked at once, each runs only its own effort.
  • Equity left = sponsor equity − problem carry. The deal places while equity left is above zero.
  • Fee = 1.5% × deal. Share = 0.75% × deal + 3% × max(0, exit value − debt).
  • Exit value = 1.3 × deal base, 0.65 × deal bad (halved), 1.6 × deal good. These multiples are page assumptions, not a forecast.
  • Diligence on a deal = $120k usual path, $30k with an AI first read, counsel review included.

This page sorts problems and shows arithmetic. It is not investment, legal or tax advice. Fee and warrant terms, and how they are taxed, depend on your engagement letter and local rules. Check those before relying on any figure.
