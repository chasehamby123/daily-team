What the gap has cost

Cold leads = leads a month × months × (contact rate with fast reply − contact rate now)
Commission missed = cold leads × bind rate × commission. Lead spend = cold leads × cost per lead.

Three weakest areas

Two paths, same inputs

The usual path is a flat answering service. The shortcut is a per-lead first text that hands off to the assigned agent in under a minute. Bad case: half the leads, and the contact rate rises less.

Added commission = leads × (new contact rate − rate now) × bind rate × commission
Per-lead net = added commission − leads × per-lead quote
Flat net = added commission − flat quote

First text: AI or a person

AI cost = leads × AI cost per lead. Person cost = leads × minutes per text ÷ 60 × wage.

AI drafts the first text from the signed script only. A person still reviews:

  • The script, signed by your compliance manager before it goes live, and again after any change.
  • Every thread. The assigned agent is copied on the first text and every reply.
  • Any reply that is not a time or a yes. It goes to the agent, not back to the AI.
  • The call itself. Only an agent quotes, advises, or binds.
  • The opt-out list, checked weekly by the owner or office manager.
