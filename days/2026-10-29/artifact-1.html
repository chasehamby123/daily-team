Owner pins

A pin sits in line once it has a quoted line and a sign-off. Until then it hangs loose and grey. Up to 10 owners.

  1. sourcing months = calls per LOI × LOIs per close ÷ calls per month
  2. months to next close = sourcing months + months LOI to close
  3. closes in 18 mo = (18 − months LOI to close) × calls per month ÷ (calls per LOI × LOIs per close). The plan holds if this is at least the add-ons planned.
  4. calls per month needed = planned × calls per LOI × LOIs per close ÷ (18 − months LOI to close)
  5. CIMs per close = CIMs per LOI × LOIs per close; by hand = CIMs × hours × $/h; with model = CIMs × (model min + sign-off min) ÷ 60 × $/h + CIMs × run $
  6. retainer path = retainer + dead LOIs × spent before break; share path = share % × price (on close only) + dead LOIs × spent before break; dead LOIs = LOIs per close − 1
  7. Mark: inside the range is level. Better than the range is ahead. Worse is behind. A row with no figure or no range stays unmarked.
