Formula

  1. Filter share f = (0.75 − 0.025 × smallest cheque in $m), × 0.75 if fund I/II is required. Kept between 5% and 95%.
  2. Met: meet everyone = names. Filter first = names × f, rounded.
  3. Committed = met × commit rate, to the nearest whole investor (ties round down).
  4. Capital = committed × average cheque.
  5. Wasted hours = (met − committed) × hours per investor.
  6. Total hours = met × hours per investor.
  7. Months to final close = months raised + 3 + ⌈total hours ÷ partner hours a month⌉. Bad +6, good −2.
  8. Retainer cost = retainer × months. Column three = small retainer × months + share × capital. The share is paid only at first close.
  9. No first close when capital is below 50% of target.
