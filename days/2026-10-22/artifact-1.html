Formula

Hourly cost        = monthly burn ÷ working hours a month
OUT summaries      = summaries a month × out-of-range share

Usual path, a month
  wasted reading   = OUT summaries × hours per summary × hourly cost
  dead diligence   = diligence fee ÷ months per dead LOI
  total            = wasted reading + dead diligence

Shortcut, a month (size checked before reading)
  OUT checks       = OUT summaries × check hours × hourly cost
  AI reads         = summaries a month × AI price per summary
  dead review      = (CPA review + AI cleanup) ÷ months per dead LOI
  total            = OUT checks + AI reads + dead review

Per year           = a month × 12
Cost so far        = a month × months searched
Slip cost          = a month × slip months
Share at close     = share % × equity share × closable capital
                     (paid once, only if a deal closes)

This is arithmetic on your own figures. It is not investment, legal, or tax advice.
