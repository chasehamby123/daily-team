What a person still does

Formula

reported hours/week = teasers × read minutes ÷ 60 review hours/week = teasers × review minutes ÷ 60 hours returned/week = reported − reported + review = teasers × (read − review) ÷ 60 hours back, 12 months = hours returned/week × working weeks value = hours back × loaded hourly cost per-teaser cost = teasers × fee × working weeks net = value − per-teaser cost success share = 0.001 × equity check × closes break point, review = read − 60 × fee ÷ hourly cost
