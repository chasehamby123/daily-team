Panel 7 · Formula

How the numbers are made

Step cost        = times paid × hours each × $ per hour
Integration      = closes × hours (owner stays or leaves) × integration $ per hour
Cost per close   = sum of the six step costs ÷ closes
Dead-deal band   = step cost × (times paid − closes) ÷ times paid
Direct path      = letters (broker calls × letters per call) at hours per letter,
                   CIM read at AI hours, diligence passes × (1 − cut %),
                   other steps as entered
Bad case         = every count before integration × 2 (close rate halves), owner leaves
Good case        = every count before integration × 0.75, owner stays
Yearly cost      = cost per close × add-ons planned in the next 12 months
Rollover line    = 20% of a $4M deal, shown, not netted against the saving

Wages, broker fees, and the tax treatment of rollover equity depend on your region and deal terms. Enter your own figures. This page does arithmetic. It is not investment, legal, or tax advice.
