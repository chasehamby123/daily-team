The shortcut: pre-screen before full diligence

Usual path: full diligence on every deal. Shortcut: an AI reads the data room first, a named analyst checks and signs what it found, and only deals that pass go to full diligence. Same deals, same costs, both paths below.

AI pre-screen time (days)

What a person still reviews. The AI reads the data room and flags. A named analyst then checks:

  • every lien and charge search against the registry,
  • the security package and who ranks ahead,
  • each EBITDA adjustment the AI flagged,
  • the pass or fail call, which the analyst signs.

Formulas

Interest            = loan × rate
Stressed EBITDA     = EBITDA × (1 + case EBITDA change)
Interest cover      = stressed EBITDA ÷ interest        walk below 1.0x
Collateral cover    = collateral ÷ loan
Recovery in a sale  = collateral × (1 − haircut) ÷ loan  walk below 100%
Cash repaid by term = min(loan, max(0, stressed EBITDA − interest) × term)
Refinance needed    = loan − cash repaid by term
Largest loan held   = min(stressed EBITDA ÷ rate, collateral × (1 − haircut))
Wait version EBITDA = EBITDA × (1 + audit adjustment)
Weeks to close      = diligence weeks + case slip (+ audit wait)
Dead-deal spend, usual      = dead deals × diligence cost
Dead-deal spend, pre-screen = dead deals × screen cost
                              + dead deals × (1 − catch rate) × diligence cost

Cash after interest ignores tax, capex and working capital. The pre-screen without AI is assumed to catch the same share of dead deals.

Arithmetic on figures you enter. This is not investment, legal or tax advice. Haircuts, lien searches and enforcement timelines depend on local rules and the security package. Enter the figures that apply to yours.
