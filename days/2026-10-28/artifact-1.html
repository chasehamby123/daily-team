Setup Sheet

Setup Sheet: twelve questions before your client signs

Ask any deal sponsor or provider these twelve questions. One A4 page. Rows 1 to 3 are stop rows. The filled sheet prints as a client memo, and only with a named reviewer and a date.

  1. Ask in order.
  2. Write the answer.
  3. Cite the page.
  4. Stop if a red row fails.

What diligence costs you each year

Two numbers. The example is a family office desk that sees 10 private deals a year at $2M a ticket.

Fees differ by firm and by country. Enter the quotes you have.

Survivors = deals x (1 - stop-row catch) Usual spend = deals x (diligence + slip share x refresh) Usual loss = die share x deals x diligence Staged spend = deals x AI read + survivors x diligence + slip share x survivors x (AI read + refresh) Staged loss = deals x AI read + die share x survivors x diligence "Loss" is money spent on deals that die after diligence. Deals per year scale both paths the same way.

Where AI does part of the work

AI reads the offering memo and data room for the three stop rows, about $1,500 a deal. A named reviewer signs the memo.

A person still does these steps

  • Checks each stop-row answer against the cited page.
  • Runs or commissions full diligence on deals that pass rows 1 to 3.
  • Asks rows 4 to 12 on the sponsor call. Budget: 25 minutes, about 2 minutes a row.
  • Signs and dates the memo. The AI does not sign.
