Two ways to hand over the name

Same inputs for both. The usual path pays at signing on today's profit. The share path pays less at signing, then a share of profit for a set number of years, with a floor in writing.

Multiples, tax on each payment, and how a floor is enforced depend on your buyer and where you are. Enter the terms you are offered. This page does not give legal, tax, or investment advice.

The formulas

Loss by reply time: 5 min 5%, 1 h 20%, 4 h 40%, 6 h 45%, 8 h+ 55%
Callers lost a month = inbound calls × loss
Bookings slipped a month = inbound calls × loss × close rate
Value slipped a month = bookings slipped × average job value
Value until handover = value slipped a month × months until you step back
Staff it could pay = value slipped a month ÷ monthly cost of one staff member, rounded down
Plate shine, month k = 1 − (bookings slipped through month k ÷ (inbound × close rate × 12)), lowest 0.25
Front desk wins back = inbound × (loss − 5%) × close rate × job value
Net = wins back − monthly cost
Margin = annual profit ÷ (inbound × (1 − loss) × close rate × job value × 12)
Profit after signing = profit at signing + (front desk wins back × 12 × margin − desk cost × 12), if counted
Usual path = usual multiple × profit at signing
Share path = share multiple × profit at signing + years × max(share % × profit after, floor × profit at signing)
Bad case: calls ÷ 2, handover + 6 months, profit at signing scales with calls
Good case: calls × 1.25, handover on schedule
