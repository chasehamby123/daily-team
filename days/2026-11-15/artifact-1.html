The assembly

Each cell is total cost to that month. Undo is the price to walk the path back at that month. Deals missed is a count. It is not in any dollar figure.

Shortcut beside the usual path

Same inputs. The usual path is an annual licence plus a fee per teaser. The shortcut is a per-teaser price with no annual contract and 30 days' notice.

With AI and without it

What the AI pass does

Extracts reported and adjusted EBITDA from each teaser. Lists each add-back with the line it came from. Flags any teaser that gives an adjusted figure with no list.

What a person still reviews

  • Checks reported against adjusted EBITDA, line by line, for any teaser that could move forward.
  • Reads footnotes the AI marks as uncertain: customer concentration, related-party revenue, one-off items.
  • A named associate signs off every teaser that moves to a call. The AI never moves a teaser to a call.
