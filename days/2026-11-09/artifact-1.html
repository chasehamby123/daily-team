Two questions

Deal size, in the deal currency. Kept in the shared link for your integration lead.

Months until the queues merge, or how long you expect the overload to run.

Formula

λ = enquiries an hour. c = people on shift. h = minutes per reply. μ = 60 ÷ h replies an hour per person. a = λ ÷ μ.

load ρ = λ × h ÷ (60 × c)

When ρ is below 100%, Erlang C:

P(wait) = C(c, a) share within t = 1 − P(wait) × e^(−(cμ − λ) × t)

When ρ is 100% or higher, a backlog that grows across an 8-hour day, starting empty:

share within a day = cμ ÷ λ share within t = min(cμ ÷ λ, t × cμ ÷ ((λ − cμ) × 8)) waiting over a day, per day = λ × 8 × (1 − share within a day)

Shortcut path. AI closes 40% of enquiries at once. People get the rest from AI drafts, a third faster:

λ people = λ × 0.6, h people = h × 2/3 share = 0.4 + 0.6 × share answered by people

Targets: 80% within 5 minutes, 95% within 1 hour, 100% within 1 day. The 1-day target counts as met at 99.5%.

staff hours a week = people on shift × 40 open hours review hours a week = λ × 0.4 × 1 min ÷ 60 × 40 FTE = staff hours ÷ contract hours

One day means 8 working hours. Each day starts with an empty queue. A queue that starts the day behind does worse than shown.
