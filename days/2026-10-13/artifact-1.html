Same arrivals for both paths. Fixed seed. Each deal has the same relative difficulty on both paths. The model clock runs continuously from Mon 09:00. ">1d" marks a note signed more than 24 hours after the enquiry.








Formula



P(T ≤ t) = 1 − e^(−(μ − λ)·t)

μ = 1 / hours per read    t = 5/60, 1, 24 hours

if λ ≥ μ: QUEUE DOES NOT CLEAR

turn-away rule: accept up to λ* = μ − ln(1 / (1 − target)) / 24, turn away λ − λ* per hour



One reader, first come first served, read times vary at random around the average (M/M/1 queue). Enquiries per quarter = λ × desk hours. Waited past a day = enquiries × (1 − P(T ≤ 24)). Cost, usual = enquiries × hours × rate. Cost, AI = enquiries × (tool cost + review hours × rate).
