Formula

Stays value = EBITDA × 1.25 × 5

Leaves value = EBITDA × 0.7 × 5

Paid if leaves = Price − Earnout

Net = Value − Paid − Integration

Good = Stays net

Base = Odds × Stays net + (1 − Odds) × Leaves net

Bad = Leaves value − Paid if leaves − 2 × Integration

Holds = Bad loss ≤ Largest loss you can absorb

Diligence = Dead deals × cost per dead deal

Diligence bad = (Dead deals + 1) × cost, AI saves half as much

Multiples of 1.25 and 0.7 on EBITDA, and the 5× value multiple, are fixed rough-pass figures. All results are pre-tax. Earnout enforceability and tax treatment depend on local rules. Enter after-tax figures if that is how you compare deals. This page does arithmetic on your figures. It is not investment, legal, or tax advice.
