Pre-read before the QoE

Usual path: engage accountants at signing and let them clean the trailing twelve months. Shortcut: AI reads the LOI for the exclusivity date and break terms and puts the trailing twelve months in order first. Accountants start only if the deal passes.

Usual, deal proceeds = QoE + cleanup Shortcut, deal proceeds = AI tool + hours × your hour + accountant review + QoE Usual, dies in week 3 = (QoE + cleanup) × share billed Shortcut, dies in week 3 = AI tool + hours × your hour (accountants never engaged)
