Stack and Clock / risk register

Enter your capital stack and the date the lender's commitment expires. Every risk to closing is ranked by dollars at risk. Every likelihood names its source.

Case drivers and rates you set

Extension fees, rates, and fees depend on your documents and local market. Enter yours.

The weakest layer

A three-by-three grid can be nine guesses dressed as data. The likelihood column is the weak part of any register, so it is the one part reworked here: each score shows the date or clause behind it. The rest stays plain.

2. Ranked register, all twelve rows

The top row is marked. Nothing is cut: a lender reads one row as eleven hidden.

Rank score = likelihood (1 to 3) × dollars at risk Clear by = week on the countdown when the risk must be closed out

3. Heat grid

Tap a cell to see each risk in it, the clause that put it there, and who reviewed it.

4. Bad, base, and good: does the close hold

Extra carry = senior debt × rate move × slip months ÷ 12 (assumes the full loan is outstanding) Extension fee = senior debt × extension fee % (applies if close slips past expiry) Holds if extra carry + extension fee ≤ contingency

5. Filling the gap: placement agent or co-GP

Same stack, same dates, same slip. Two ways to fill the equity gap.

Agent path cost = gap × placement fee % + retainer If the deal dies: retainer + your diligence Co-GP path cost = projected promote × co-GP share % If the deal dies: your diligence (co-GP carries its own)

6. First read of the documents: AI pass or counsel

An AI pass reads the term sheet and loan commitment and lists every clause it flags, low-confidence ones included. Counsel still signs off. Counsel rates vary by market; enter yours.

Base, with AI = AI pass + counsel review Base, without = counsel full read Bad, with AI = AI pass + counsel review + counsel full read (AI misreads a clause, counsel reads it all anyway) Bad, without = counsel full read

A person reviews: every flagged clause, every likelihood score and its reason, the dollar impacts, and the final sign-off. The AI drafts the list. It does not decide what is in or out.
