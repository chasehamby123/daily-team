The words, defined before the numbers

Successor
Whoever takes over the firm. On this page: your own people, or someone funded by a bank loan.
Setup
What the successor pays on day one.
Monthly cost
What the successor pays out each month. For your own people this is the earn-out: a share of profit paid to you.
Monthly gain
What the business earns the successor each month, before they pay you or the bank.
Breaks even
The first month the successor has earned back their setup.
Paid in full
The month the agreed price has reached you.
Survives
The successor breaks even by the month you set below, in the bad case.
