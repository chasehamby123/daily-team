Fee path vs share path

Same inputs, two ways to pay for diligence. The fee path pays an analyst up front. The share path pays nothing for full diligence until close: an AI-first read up front, then a share of carry only on deals that close.

Bad case: deal volume halved, fall-through doubled, closes slip 6 months. Good case: volume up 25%, fall-through cut by 40%.

Assumptions (example figures; enter the quotes you have)

Formula

n       = deals to diligence per year x volume factor
dead    = n x fall-through
closed  = n - dead
S       = ticket x profit multiple x carry rate x share of carry
FEE     cash now = n x fee           sunk = dead x fee
SHARE   cash now = n x read          sunk = dead x read
        on close = closed x S        paid at exit, later by any slip
HOLDS   when, in the bad case, SHARE total <= FEE total
        and SHARE sunk <= FEE sunk
BREAK   S* = (fee - read) / (1 - bad fall-through)
BACKLOG unvetted after 12 months = deals brought - 12 x vet rate
