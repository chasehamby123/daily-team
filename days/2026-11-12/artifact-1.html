What They'll Open First

A buyer checks six things before talking price. Mark what is ready. The gaps become prep hours. Then compare a flat fee against a small base plus a share of the price above your own floor. No valuation is suggested. The price you enter stays yours.

Step 2. Your numbers

Floor, price and terms

Floor B is the lowest price you would accept. Expected price P is your own figure. Fees exclude VAT, and tax on the sale is not counted. Both depend on local rules. Check them with your accountant.

Who loses what
