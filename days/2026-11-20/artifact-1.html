Two ways to answer the night line

Same inputs on both sides. The bad case sits under each.

Usual path: hire a night dispatcher

Wage plus payroll tax. Wage and overtime rules vary by state. Enter your local figure.

  • Setup: post the job, interview, train. Weeks.
  • A person answers, books, and can talk price if you let them.
  • Cost stays fixed when calls drop.

Side door: forward nights to an AI answering service

Enter the quote you have. This page quotes nothing.

  • Setup: dial your carrier's forward code once. About 2 minutes. Codes vary by carrier.
  • The AI answers, takes name and address, and books an open slot in the calendar you already use. It does not diagnose.
  • Owner approves every price and urgency text.
  • Call recording and consent rules vary by state. Check yours before recording is on.
