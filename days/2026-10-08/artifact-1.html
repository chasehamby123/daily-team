Cheque First

Paste this morning's investor list. Names below your minimum cheque or with no past commitment to your strategy come off first. The rest sort into four bands. The page ends with one name to call today.

  1. Export the list from the CRM.
  2. Paste it below. Answer two questions.
  3. Read down to the first call.

The list

Columns: name, typical cheque in $m, past commitments to your strategy, partner hours so far, associate flag (dead or warm), signed (initials). A cell ending in ? was filled by AI and is not checked yet. Initials in the last column sign the row. A dead flag from the associate overrides every score.














Assumptions (defaults, change to your own)

Fee terms and the value of an hour depend on your firm, your agent's contract and local rules. Enter your own.
