Why the score is weighted

A plain 0–15 count treats "contact email present" and "no spelling errors" as equal. They are not. Each check here has a weight of 1, 2 or 3. Five checks weigh 3, five weigh 2, five weigh 1. Maximum: 5×3 + 5×2 + 5×1 = 30. The rest of the brief is unchanged: 15 checks, one page, printable.
